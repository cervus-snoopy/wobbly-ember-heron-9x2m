## The line

> ERROR: I wobble at speed 3. I have wobbled since 2019. You call it character. I call it a loose screw.

102 characters. A ceiling fan reports its own fault — a wobble at speed 3 from a loose screw — in its own flat, put-upon voice. The laugh comes out of the truth of the fault, not a pun bolted on.
